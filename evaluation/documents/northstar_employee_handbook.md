# Northstar Employee Handbook

This synthetic handbook is provided only as a reusable RAG evaluation fixture. Northstar
Analytics is a fictional organization.

## Work Schedule

Full-time employees work 40 hours each week. Teams may arrange their remaining hours
flexibly, but all employees must be available for the core collaboration window from
10:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m. Eastern Time on Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday. Remote work
is permitted within the employee's approved country of employment.

## Time Away

Employees receive 18 paid-time-off days and 8 paid sick days each calendar year. Up to
5 unused PTO days may carry into the next calendar year. Sick days do not carry over.
This carryover rule applies only to PTO; it does not increase the annual sick-day grant.

## Health and Retirement Benefits

Medical coverage becomes effective on the first day of the month after 30 calendar days
of employment. Retirement-plan eligibility begins after 90 calendar days of employment.
Northstar matches 100 percent of the first 3 percent of eligible pay contributed by an
employee, plus 50 percent of the next 2 percent contributed. Employer matching
contributions vest immediately.

## Business Travel and Expenses

Travel must be approved at least 10 calendar days before departure.

## Information Security

Suspected security incidents must be reported immediately to the Security Operations
Center at extension 7711 and to security@northstar.example. Employees should preserve
relevant messages or files and must not investigate the incident on their own.
